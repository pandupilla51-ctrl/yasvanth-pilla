# 🌱 AgriBridge AI

### AI-Powered Smart Farming & Precision Agriculture Platform

**AgriBridge AI** is an intelligent agriculture platform that combines **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, IoT, weather intelligence, soil monitoring, and farm analytics** to help farmers make smarter and data-driven decisions.

## 🚀 Implemented Features

* 🔬 **AI Leaf Disease Diagnosis** — Upload a crop leaf image and analyze visible symptoms, crop condition, confidence, severity, likely cause, and recommended management.

* 🤖 **AI Agronomist Assistant** — Get AI-powered agricultural guidance for crop health, disease management, treatment, prevention, and farming-related queries.

* 💧 **Smart Irrigation Monitoring** — Monitor soil moisture, irrigation conditions, water requirements, and irrigation recommendations.

* 🌦️ **Weather & Climate Intelligence** — View weather conditions and farming-focused climate insights to support crop and irrigation decisions.

* 🧪 **Soil Health Monitoring** — Track soil parameters and receive insights related to soil and crop health.

* 🗺️ **Farm Spatial Map** — Visualize farm plots, crop information, field conditions, irrigation areas, and sensor-related data.

* 📈 **Agricultural Market Insights** — Monitor crop market information, prices, and trends to support better selling decisions.

* 📊 **Farm Analytics Dashboard** — Centralized visualization of crop health, soil, weather, irrigation, market, and AI-generated insights.

* 🔔 **Smart Alerts & Recommendations** — Provides important farming alerts and actionable recommendations based on available farm conditions.

* 🌐 **Modern Responsive Interface** — Designed with a professional agricultural dashboard experience and responsive UI for different screen sizes.

## 🎯 Project Goal

AgriBridge AI aims to bridge the gap between **AI technology and practical agriculture** by providing farmers with intelligent tools for **crop monitoring, disease identification, irrigation management, soil analysis, weather insights, and agricultural decision-making**.

> **See the problem → Understand the problem → Get AI insights → Take smarter action.**
